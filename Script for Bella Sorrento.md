# Bella Sorrento: call script (Fri 16 Oct, 14:45)

**Number:** 07522 199382 · **Preview:** bella-sorrento-preview.vercel.app
**Know before dialling:** 4.6★ from 225 Google reviews · Website button on Google goes to their Facebook · orders go through Uber Eats · closed Tuesdays · 14:45 is the quiet gap between lunch and dinner.

**Goal of the call:** get the owner to open the link on WhatsApp while you're on the phone (or agree to look tonight).

---

## 1. Opener
> "Hi, is that the owner? … It's Stef from Kose Web, I'm a local web developer. Have you got two minutes? I'll be quick, I know you'll be prepping for tonight."

**Not the owner:** "No worries, who's best to speak to about the restaurant's website, and when are they in?" → write down name + time.
**Bad time:** "Totally understand. Is tomorrow morning or after the lunch rush better?" → book it.

## 2. Why you're calling
> "I was looking you up on Google: 4.6 stars from 225 reviews, people go on about the portions and the pizza. But when someone taps 'Website' it just goes to your Facebook page. So people deciding where to eat can't easily see the menu, the prices or the hours."

## 3. The hook: it's already built
> "So I actually went ahead and built you one, as an example of what it could look like. It's got your full pizza menu with prices, your hours, a map and a call button. It's not live on Google or anything, it's just a private link. Can I WhatsApp it to you now so you can have a look while we're talking?"

→ **Send straight away:**
> Hi, it's Stef from Kose Web. Here's the preview I made for Bella Sorrento: https://bella-sorrento-preview.vercel.app

## 4. While they look (pause, let them scroll)
- "That's how it looks on a phone, which is where most people will see it."
- "The photos are just placeholders. I'd swap them for photos of your actual food."
- "Prices are from your Uber Eats menu. I'd add the pasta, risotto, breakfasts and waffles properly."
- "Tap 'Call to order' and it rings you directly. The bit at the bottom shows open or closed automatically."

Then **stop talking** and ask:
> "What do you think?"

## 5. If they like it → close
> "Brilliant. So it's £395 to set it up, then £39 a month, which covers hosting, your domain name and any changes, like new prices or specials. Or there's no upfront cost at all and it's £69 a month for the first year. It'd be live in about a week, linked to your Google listing."

**If they hesitate on price (use the founding offer):**
> "Actually, you'd be one of my first clients round here, so I can do it for £195 and £29 a month, if you're happy for me to show it as an example and leave a quick review."

**If yes:**
1. "Great. What's your name, and is this the best number for WhatsApp?"
2. "Can you send me your full menu and some photos of the food? Phone photos are fine."
3. "I'll send over a short agreement and an invoice by email. What's the best email?"
4. "Once that's sorted, I'll have it live within the week."

## 6. Objections

**"We've got Facebook."**
> "Facebook's great for your regulars. But on Google, the Website button's the first thing people tap, and Facebook makes them hunt for the menu. The site would link to your Facebook too, so you keep both."

**"We use Uber Eats."**
> "Keep it, it's great for delivery. The website's for the people who'd rather call you or walk in, and those orders don't lose Uber's cut. Later on I can add your own online ordering if you want, but that's separate."

**"We're busy enough."**
> "That's a good problem! It'd also cut down the calls asking 'are you open?' or 'how much is a pizza?', because it's all on there."

**"Too expensive."**
> "Fair. Think of it as about £1.30 a day. If it brings in one extra table a week, it's paid for itself. And there's the no-upfront option."

**"Who are you? What experience have you got?"**
> "I'm local, I've been lead developer at a few financial market-data companies, including my own, FreeFlow. A restaurant site's a lot simpler than that, and you've already seen the work before paying anything."

**"Let me think about it." / "I'll talk to my partner."**
> "Of course. The link stays up, so have a look tonight. Can I give you a ring Monday at the same time?" → book it. (They're closed Tuesday.)

**"Not interested."**
> "No problem at all, thanks for your time. I'll leave the link with you in case you change your mind."

## 7. No answer / voicemail
Hang up, don't leave a message, don't send the link cold. Ring back in a quiet slot:
- **Best:** Mon, Wed or Thu, 2:30–4:30pm (between lunch and dinner)
- **Also OK:** around 10am after the breakfast rush
- **Avoid:** Tuesdays (closed), 12–2pm, after 5pm, Friday and Saturday evenings
- After 3 tries with no answer, consider popping in on a quiet weekday afternoon with the site open on your phone.

Only send the link once you've spoken to them and they've said yes to seeing it.

## Write down during the call
Owner's name · WhatsApp number · email · what they liked / want changed · plan chosen · follow-up date/time
