# Call script: Florists & gifts

Use with the **Florists & gifts** tab. Fill in the [brackets] from the lead's row.

**Best time to call:** Mid-morning on weekdays. Avoid the weeks before Valentine's Day and Mother's Day.
**Tip:** Timing pitch: get it live before Christmas / Valentine's / Mother's Day.

**Before dialling (30 seconds)**
1. Google the exact name + town. If they have a website, mark Checked = N and skip them.
2. Check the number isn't on TPS (tpsonline.org.uk).
3. Note their **rating**, **reviews**, and **website status** (none / Facebook-Instagram-booking page only / spam link).
4. Skim 2 reviews for something specific: people usually mention how beautiful the flowers were, how long they lasted.

---

## 1. Opener
> "Hi, is that [owner's name / the owner]? … It's [your name] from Kose Web. I'm a local web developer here in Suffolk. Have you got two minutes? I'll be quick."

- **Not the owner:** "No worries. Who's best to speak to about the shop's website, and when are they usually around?" → note the name and time.
- **Bad time:** "No problem. Is tomorrow morning or afternoon better?" → book it in Notes.

## 2. Hook
> "I was looking you up on Google: [rating] stars from [reviews] reviews, and people really rate [thing from a review]. But when someone taps 'Website', [there's nothing there / it just goes to your Facebook / it goes to a page that isn't even yours]."

## 3. Problem
> "So when people search something like 'florist in Halesworth' or 'flowers delivered Suffolk', they can't see photos of your bouquets, prices, and whether you deliver, and a lot of them just tap on the next one that has a proper site."

## 4. Offer
> "I build simple, proper websites for local businesses like yours: a bouquet gallery with prices, wedding and funeral flowers, your delivery area and an order or enquiry form. It's linked to your Google listing and looks great on a phone. I'll make you a free preview, no obligation, so you can see exactly what yours would look like."

## 5. The ask
> "Can I put that together and send it to you on WhatsApp by [day, within 48 hours]? If you don't like it, no worries at all."

**If yes:**
1. "What's your name, and is this the best number for WhatsApp?"
2. "Can you send me your best bouquet photos, your delivery area and wedding or funeral work? Phone photos are fine."
3. "I'll send it by [day] and give you a quick ring after you've had a look."
4. Outcome = **Interested - send preview**. Build it, deploy it, then do the follow-up call below.

## Objections you'll hear in florists & gifts

**"We use Interflora / Facebook."**
> "Keep it. Your own site has no commission, and it's where you show your wedding and funeral work properly."

**"It's a quiet time of year."**
> "That's exactly the time to do it, so it's live and on Google before the big days."

## Objections you'll hear in every industry

**"We've been doing fine without one."**
Agree first, and never argue.
> "Honestly, that's clear: [reviews] reviews at [rating] stars doesn't happen by accident. The site isn't about fixing anything. It's about the people who've never heard of you. Your regulars already know you."

> "Can I ask, where do most of your new customers come from at the minute?" (let them answer) "Right, and when someone gets recommended you, the first thing they do now is Google you. A proper page makes you look like the safe pick."

> "That's why I'd rather show you than tell you. It's free, and if you look at it and think 'nah', no harm done."

If they're still firm: "No worries. Can I check back in a few months?" → Not interested, and put the date in Notes.

**"I've got Facebook."**
> "Facebook's great for regulars. On Google, though, the Website button is what people tap first, and Facebook makes them hunt. The site links to your Facebook too."

**"How much?" (only give prices when asked)**
> "It's £395 to set it up, then £39 a month. That covers hosting, your domain name, and small changes like new prices or photos. Or there's no upfront cost and it's £69 a month for the first year. Either way the preview's free, so you see it first."

*If they hesitate (first few clients only):* "You'd be one of my first clients round here, so I can do £195 and £29 a month if you're happy for me to show it as an example and leave a quick review."

**"Too expensive."**
> "Fair. It's about £1.30 a day. One extra customer a month covers it, and there's the no-upfront option."

**"Who are you? What experience have you got?"**
> "We're local. I've been lead developer at a few financial market-data companies, including my own, FreeFlow. A site like this is a lot simpler, and you see the preview before paying anything."

**"Send me something."**
> "That's exactly what the preview is. What's your WhatsApp? I'll send it by [day]."

**"What if I want to cancel?"**
> "12 months minimum, then month to month with 30 days' notice. The domain's in your name, and if you leave I hand it all over."

**"Not interested."**
> "No problem at all, thanks for your time." → Outcome = Not interested.

## Preview follow-up call (after sending the link)
> "Hi [name], it's [your name] from Kose Web. I've just sent your preview on WhatsApp. Have you got a sec to open it while we're on the phone?"
1. Let them scroll, and point out the bits that matter to them.
2. "The photos are placeholders. I'd swap in yours."
3. Ask "What do you think?", then **stop talking**.
4. If yes: give the price, get their email for the agreement and invoice. Outcome = **Won**.

## Rules
- **No answer:** no voicemail, no cold link. Try again at another good time. After 3 tries, Outcome = No answer.
- Only send a link once they've agreed to see it.
- Log every call: Called date, Outcome, Notes (owner's name and the best time to call).
- Be honest: never say you're "from Google" or imply they have to do this.
